Our story

Made slowly.
Worn with intent.

Maison Noir began in a small distillery where Ittar was made the old way: copper stills, wood fires, and patience measured in months. We carry that heritage into perfumery written for men who prefer to be remembered rather than noticed.

Each blend is aged, filtered by hand and tested on skin before it leaves our workshop.
